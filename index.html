About

Looking for the
honest moment.

I'm Oscar, a photographer based in Austin, Texas. I shoot with a Sony A7 IV — a camera that gets out of the way and lets me focus on what's in front of me.

My work spans portraits, travel, and street photography. I'm drawn to available light, quiet compositions, and the kind of moments that pass before you've decided to look.

Currently building this portfolio. New work added regularly.
